1. WHY (user decision 2026-09-28)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The prompt is part of our approach's contribution. The current "pure
inference" baselines (pure_inference/offline_inference_fine_tuned_model_
extended_experiments.py, config pure_inference/config/qwen3_*_clccd.yaml)
use a prompt ADAPTED FROM OUR OWN mcts_prompt.json, so they already contain
part of our contribution. Headline comparisons (e.g. Qwen3-4B + our approach
vs Qwen3-8B) must instead be against the BEST PROMPTS FROM THE LITERATURE.
"Our prompt, single pass" stays in the paper as an ABLATION step:
  literature prompt -> our prompt -> + execution/search -> + SFT.
